Educational Guide

YOUR BABY'S POSITION IN THE WOMB

Different positions, same beautiful journey.

Worried about your baby's position? Our Mother and Baby healthcare providers can assess and address your unique situation with prenatal ultrasound and hands-on Leopold's maneuvers.

Optimal Position 01

OCCIPUT ANTERIOR

Head is down and the back is toward the front of the mother's body. This is the most favorable and common position for a smooth vaginal birth.

Face Up 02

OCCIPUT POSTERIOR

Head is down and the back is toward the back of the mother's body ("sunny-side up"). May cause prolonged back labor; our staff helps guide position changes.

Horizontal 03

TRANSVERSE LIE

The baby lies horizontally across the mother's uterus, with the head on one side and the buttocks on the other. Ultrasound confirms this alignment.

Breech Variant 04

COMPLETE BREECH

Buttocks are pointed toward the birth canal with the baby's legs bent at the knees and feet near the buttocks (cross-legged position).

Feet First 05

FOOTLING BREECH

One or both feet are pointed downward toward the birth canal, positioned to emerge first. Requires specialized obstetric delivery pathways.

Buttocks First 06

FRANK BREECH

Buttocks are down toward the cervix and the baby's feet are extended straight up near their chest and face.
